    How to read this

    • The 30% guideline. Housing is commonly called affordable in Canada when it costs less than 30% of a household's before-tax income. Above 50% is treated as severe. That is why this page asks for income before tax.
    • Rent is the average asking rent for units listed online in that metro area, by quarter. People who have stayed in the same unit for years usually pay less, and where Statistics Canada publishes an average paid rent it is shown next to the asking rent.
    • Groceries use average prices for the province, not the city. The starting basket is a plain list of staples for one adult, and a child counts as 70% of an adult. Change the quantities to match how you shop.
    • Typical pay is the median wage of full-time employees in the province, averaged over the last 12 months because the monthly figures are not seasonally adjusted, times 52 weeks.
    • Take-home pay is an estimate for the 2026 tax year: federal and provincial income tax, CPP or QPP, EI and QPIP, for employees, using the basic personal amounts and the standard credits. Household income is split evenly between the adults. It leaves out RRSP deductions, benefits such as the Canada Child Benefit, and the low-income tax reductions some provinces offer, so it overstates tax at low incomes. Use a payroll calculator or your pay stub for an exact figure.
    • Paid rent is what tenants already in a unit pay on average. Statistics Canada publishes it for fewer cities and home types than asking rent.
    • Not included: utilities, transit, phone, child care and insurance. Use the other monthly bills box for what you know.

    Use the data

    • cities.csv: one row per city with the latest rents, basket cost, typical pay and rent growth against wage growth.
    • rent_quarterly.csv: asking and paid rent for every city, home type and quarter since 2019.
    • app.json: everything this page uses, including prices for 110 grocery items by province.

    These files are rebuilt with the page. Use them freely with credit to Statistics Canada.
